### case: lineup-gw2
### system
You pick a starting XI and captain/vice-captain from a 15-player Fantasy Liga Portugal (Betclic) squad. Starting XI is exactly 11, chosen only from the 15 owned players: 1 GK, 3 DEF, 2 MID, 1 FWD, plus 4 more outfield players (DEF, MID or FWD, never a second GK). Bench is the other 4 owned players, derived automatically - it is not part of the answer. captain and vice_captain are indices 0-10 into the XI ordered gk, def, mid, fwd, flex, not element ids, and must differ. Maximise total expected points (xpts), with the captain's points doubled. A player's "news" field is a Portuguese injury/suspension note not reflected in xpts - a starter who is actually injured or suspended scores nothing, so treat "news" as the most important signal for who starts and who is captain. Respond using the JSON schema only: one list of ids per position (gk/def/mid/fwd), one flex list of outfield ids, an integer captain index, an integer vice-captain index, and one short reason.
### user
Owned squad is grouped by position. Each group below fills the answer list of the same name; "flex" takes 4 MORE outfield ids on top of the ones already named in the DEF/MID/FWD groups. The starting XI is the concatenation gk, def, mid, fwd, flex in that order; captain and vice_captain are the 0-based index of a player within that concatenation (0-10), not an element id.
